Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the free tier work?

Scroll Quran offers daily free reflections. After exploring 8-10 verses, you can unlock lifetime unbounded access with a one-time purchase. There are never any recurring subscriptions or advertisements.

How do I restore my Lifetime purchase on another device?

Open the app, tap the Lifetime Pro button at the top right (or in settings), and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed into the same Apple ID, your lifetime status will activate immediately.

Can I listen to Quran recitations offline?

Yes. When recitations by Sheikh Mishary Alafasy are played, they are cached locally on your device for seamless offline listening during travel or flights.

Are there any advertisements in the app?

No. The Quran is sacred scripture; Scroll Quran is and will always remain 100% ad-free.
