Scroll Quran — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Scroll Quran. Your privacy and spiritual peace of mind are sacred to us. This app is engineered from the ground up to be a quiet, distraction-free sanctuary.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, location coordinates, or advertising identifiers.

2. 100% Offline-First Local Storage

All your bookmarks, reading progress, and customized preferences (such as selected color themes and font sizes) remain strictly on your physical device via local storage. No user accounts or servers are involved.

3. In-App Purchases

Our $14.99 Lifetime Pro purchase is processed directly through Apple's official App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive or store any credit card or financial information.

4. Audio Streaming & Caching

Quran recitations are streamed on-demand from public CDNs (EveryAyah / Quran.com API) and cached locally in your device's browser/app storage for seamless offline reflection. No tracking cookies are attached.
